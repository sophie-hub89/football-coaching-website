Our Aim

Helping beginners enjoy and learn football.

Everyone Has To Start Somewhere

Our main aim is to introduce young people to football in a friendly, fun and supportive way.

We don't expect players to already know everything about football. Our sessions give beginners the chance to learn the basics and gradually become more confident.

Learn

Learn the basic skills needed to play football, including passing, shooting and dribbling.

Enjoy

Make football enjoyable through fun activities, games and positive coaching.

Grow

Build confidence, make friends and develop new skills along the way.

Thinking About Starting Football?

You don't need to be an expert. Just come along and give it a try.
